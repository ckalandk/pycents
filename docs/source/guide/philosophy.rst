==========
Philosophy
==========

**PyCents** was designed with these rules and hope that people who wants to contribute
embrasse this rules.

We made to the PEP 20 idioms:

* Beautiful is better than ugly.
* Explicit is better than implicit.
* Simple is better than complex.
* Complex is better than complicated.
* Readability counts.


Convenience at the boundaries; invariants in the core; fail fast when crossing the boundary.
Don't hide important domain decisions.
Don't invent abstractions when Python already has a good one.
When an established Python design fits, prefer familiarity over novelty.
